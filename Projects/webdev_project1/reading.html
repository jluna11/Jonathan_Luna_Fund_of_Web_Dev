Why Reading?

Reading is incredible to say the least. Not only does it improve one's own intellectual skills but also broadens one's perspective and enhances creativity in general. It's generally a great time consumer, too! Try it yourself one day when you want to make yourself busy but also be productive. You'll notice that time will zoom past and you'll be left satisfied knowing you were productive and that the time you wanted to go through went by so quickly. Additionally, to me, reading is wonderful; there's really nothing like it. Being able to use my imagination when reading is fun! It's as if I'm taking myself onto an adventure and experiencing the story firsthand. Really, it's just incredible. I highly recommend to try reading! It can be hard to, I understand, but once you finally pick up a favorite book and begin reading, you'll start to see what I mean.

Book genres:

Here are some notable book genres! Look through them and find your favorite(s).

  • Fiction
  • Non-Fiction
  • Horror

There are many more genres out there to explore--these are just a few to name. Genres can even be more specific such as Non-Fiction horror and vice versa. The possibilities are quite endless. My personal favorite is horror-fiction. Hope you're able to find your personal favorite!

Notable Book authors:

  • Stephen King, the king of horror and most notable for "The Shining"
  • J.K. Rowling, the creator and author of the world-famous "Harry Potter" series
  • R.L. Stine, the author of the "Goosebumps" series (my personal favorite series)
  • J.D. Salinger, the author of "The Catcher in the Rye" (my personal favorite book)
  • Suzanne Collins, the author of "The Hunger Games" series

As you can see, there are many talented authors out there. These aren't even all of the popular/notable ones. You don't even necessarily have to read books from popular authors, too. Supporting smaller one's is a great way to also explore newly released books! You never know, you might find a hidden gem.

Conclusion:

In conclusion, reading is a lovely hobby to have. It can be a great way to pass the time, improve intellectual skills, and open up new perspectives on life. As previously stated, I highly recommend you start soon! You won't regret it.
