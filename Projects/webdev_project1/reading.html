Why Reading?

Reading is incredible to say the least. Not only does it improve one's own intellectual skills but also broadens one's perspective and enhances creativity in general. It's generally a great time consumer, too! Try it yourself one day when you want to make yourself busy but also be productive. You'll notice that time will zoom past and you'll be left satisfied knowing you were productive and that the time you wanted to go through went by so quickly. Additionally, to me, reading is wonderful; there's really nothing like it. Being able to use my imagination when reading is fun! It's as if I'm taking myself onto an adventure and experiencing the story firsthand. Really, it's just incredible. I highly recommend to try reading! It can be hard to, I understand, but once you finally pick up a favorite book and begin reading, you'll start to see what I mean. I promise.
